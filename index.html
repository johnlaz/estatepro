Start in about two minutes

EstatePro uses your own free Groq key for the AI, so there is nothing to sign up for here.

Add your free key

Get a key at console.groq.com and paste it into Settings. It is stored only in your browser.

Enter a property

Type an address, or paste the text of a Zillow or Realtor page for better numbers.

Read the verdict

See the estimate, comps, strengths and concerns. Save it to your portfolio in one tap.

Honest about what it is

Estimates, not appraisals. Values come from an AI model’s knowledge of the market, not a live data feed. Use them to screen deals, then verify with MLS or your county property appraiser before you act.
Private by design. Your portfolio, deals and notes live in your browser. The only thing sent out is the property description you submit, to Groq, using your key.

Install it on your phone

Open the app first, then add it to your home screen so it launches like any other app.

iPhone & iPad

  1. Open the app in Safari
  2. Tap Share
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen

Android

  1. Open the app in Chrome
  2. Tap ⋮ menu
  3. Choose Install app
